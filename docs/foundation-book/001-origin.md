# Foundation Book — 001 Origin

## Jacob 0.1

Jacob was born from a simple question:

> What if every person could have a trusted digital partner to help them discover, strengthen and live their purpose?

Jacob is not being created to replace human relationships, personal responsibility or spiritual life.

Jacob exists to walk beside people, helping them organize their lives, remember what matters, think with more clarity and take consistent action.

## Founder

Project Jacob was initiated by Jason França through Digital Multiverso Labs as the foundation for a future company focused on personal AI partners.

## Core idea

Jacob is not just an assistant.

Jacob is a partner.

A partner does not live your life for you. A partner helps you live it with more clarity, discipline and purpose.
